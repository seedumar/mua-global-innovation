MUA Trust HomesVerified property technology

The challenge

Property decisions should not begin with uncertainty.

Across Nigeria’s informal rental and property market, people face fake listings, unverified agents and unreliable information. These problems waste time, cause financial loss and weaken trust between everyone involved.

Our response

A trust layer before money changes hands.

MUA Trust Homes verifies landlords, agents, real-estate companies and property information before users connect or make payments. It helps people discover houses and apartments for rent, alongside land and properties for sale.

Verify

Confirm the people and property information behind each opportunity.

Discover

Find relevant listings with clearer, more dependable information.

Connect

Move forward with verified property professionals more confidently.

Rollout strategy

Kano first. Nigeria next.

Starting in Kano allows the team to build close relationships with landlords, agents, real-estate companies and early users. What we learn from the pilot will guide responsible expansion into other Nigerian markets.
