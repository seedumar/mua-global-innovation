Before you apply

Your questions, answered.

Is this a salaried job?

No fixed monthly salary is offered. Compensation is commission-based, subject to the written terms agreed before appointment. Income is not guaranteed.

What is the commission rate?

The rate and calculation basis will be disclosed and agreed in writing before you accept an appointment. Do not assume a percentage or start work on the basis of an estimate.

Can ambassadors collect client payments?

Clients should pay MUA through its approved company payment channels. Ambassadors must not collect payments into personal accounts or make unauthorised commitments on MUA’s behalf.

Does registration guarantee an appointment?

No. MUA reviews applications, shortlists suitable candidates and issues appointment letters only after selection and agreement on the terms.

Will MUA pay my travel or promotional expenses?

Do not assume expenses will be reimbursed. Obtain written approval before incurring costs on behalf of MUA.
