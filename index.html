Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Know what to expect before starting your project.

Ask us a question ↗
How do I request a quotation?

Choose a service and complete the enquiry steps. WhatsApp will open with your message prepared; review it and tap send. We will discuss the scope before preparing a quotation.

What determines the cost and delivery timeline?

The required pages or features, available content, equipment, location and scope affect the quotation. Project costs and a delivery plan are agreed before work begins.

Can MUA help with hosting and a domain name?

Yes. We can discuss domain registration, hosting and launch support as part of your website proposal. Renewal charges and responsibilities should be confirmed in the project terms.

Is maintenance included?

Maintenance and ongoing support depend on the agreed scope. Ask us to specify what is included at handover and what needs a separate support arrangement.

Can training be tailored to our staff or students?

Tell us the learners’ current level, goals and group size. We will discuss a suitable training scope and format.

How do installation projects begin?

For networking, CCTV and solar, start by sharing the location and what you need. Site details or an assessment may be needed before equipment, installation scope and cost can be confirmed.
